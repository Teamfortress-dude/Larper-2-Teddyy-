# Larper-2 "Teddyy"

The Larper-2 is a J-Class rocket motor producing around 1068 Ns of impulse and 710 Newtons of thrust. It is the second rocket motor in my Larper series and is better in every way compared to the Larper-1. The nickname Teddyy was given by a friend of mine. What differentiates itself from other amateur KNSB rocket motors is the experimental thermal insulators that use a carbon fiber reinforced 3D printing filament known as PET-CF17. This allows me to make very percise insulator sizes without making a mess by using epoxy and kraft paper.

This rocket motor is just one of many passion projects I have in mind to build a portfolio for my dream career of being a propulsion engineer. At the same time, I want to introduce to my home country of Indonesia to amateur rocketry. Currently, it is an absolute pain to even get a static fire of a rocket motor because here, amateur rocketry is regulated similary to weapons which it is obivously not. I hope this project will bring enough awarness so aspiring rocketeers can actually live out their dreams.

In terms of actual technical data, there is an uploaded PDF that gives a summary on all the simulated characteristics. If you have any questions regarding the specific math equations used to design this rocket motor, you can check the JOURNAL.MD file or email me at justinbmcclure@gmail.com. The grain casting equipment and thrust stand will each have their own repository and once everything is complete I will have a YouTube video explaining everything. I'll update this readme once the video is complete.
